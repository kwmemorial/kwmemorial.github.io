Profit Share and Growth Share, explained.

Keller Williams shares profits with the agents who help it grow. Here is how Profit Share and Growth Share work, in plain language.

How Profit Share works

When agents close deals, part of each commission goes to their market center until they reach their annual cap. The market center uses that revenue to cover its operating expenses, and a significant portion of what remains is shared with agents who helped the market center grow by attracting productive agents to Keller Williams.

When a new agent joins and names the agent who introduced them, that agent becomes their sponsor. Profit Share can extend across seven levels, so agents can benefit from the growth they help create over time.

How Growth Share works

Each month, market centers send a portion of their revenue to Keller Williams and to their region. Growth Share lets participating agents share in their region's results, adding another way to benefit from the growth of the KW network.

Why it matters

Commissions end when you stop selling. Profit Share and Growth Share reward the relationships you build and the agents you bring to Keller Williams, and KW describes this as income that can continue after your last deal closes.

Questions agents ask

What is Keller Williams Profit Share?

Profit Share is how Keller Williams market centers share a significant portion of their profits with the agents who helped them grow by attracting productive agents to Keller Williams.

What is the difference between Profit Share and Growth Share?

Profit Share comes from your market center's profits. Growth Share lets participating agents share in their region's results.

Do I have to recruit agents to succeed at KW?

No. Your business is built on your own clients and transactions. Profit Share and Growth Share are an additional way to benefit when agents you introduce join Keller Williams.

How do I start earning Profit Share?

When an agent you introduce joins Keller Williams and names you as their sponsor, you begin to share in the growth you helped create. We'll walk you through how it works in your conversation with us.
