How to grow your real estate business in Houston.

The main ways established agents grow, and the people and tools at Keller Williams Memorial behind each one.

You can grow a real estate business in a handful of ways: more listings, a higher price point, a specialty, a stronger brand, more referrals, more time for clients and income beyond your own sales. Most agents who grow do several at once. Here's each path, where to go deeper, and how Memorial helps.

Seven ways to grow

  1. Grow the listing side

    Listings market you to a neighborhood, bring in buyer leads and lead to the next seller. At Memorial, Listings to Leads markets every listing, your listings are syndicated to 80+ home search websites with your name on them, and you get customizable listing presentations, scripts and coaching. How to get more listings.

  2. Raise your price point

    Each step up in price brings more income per transaction and clients who refer at the same level. At Memorial, the Open House Exchange, mentors and our Luxury Division help you move up one step at a time. How to sell higher-priced homes, then how to become a luxury agent.

  3. Add a specialty

    A specialty gives clients a reason to choose you and opens new sources of business. At Memorial, our Luxury and International divisions meet regularly, and agents can grow into commercial work with KW Commercial Texas. See the Luxury Division, the International Division and commercial careers.

  4. Build your brand and visibility

    Buyers and sellers look you up before they call. Video, social media and a consistent brand keep you in front of them. At Memorial, the Memorial Ave content lab, an in-house headshot photographer, marketing support and KW Marketing Bar do it with you. Social media for agents and video and podcast marketing.

  5. Grow referrals and repeat business

    Past clients and their friends are the steadiest source of business. At Memorial, KW Command keeps your database organized, 100 Days to Greatness builds a referral system for seasoned agents as well as new ones, and KW's global network sends and receives referrals around the world.

  6. Get time back for clients

    Every hour you spend on paperwork is an hour you aren't with clients. At Memorial, free transaction coordinators, a contracts hotline, BrokerBot for on-demand transaction support and in-house marketing support take work off your plate.

  7. Build wealth beyond your own sales

    Income that doesn't depend on your next closing gives your business a different shape. At Memorial, Profit Share and Growth Share reward the agents you help bring to Keller Williams, and seminars cover investment, tax, retirement and estate planning. Profit Share and Growth Share.

Questions agents ask

How do I grow my real estate business?

Pick one or two paths and work them consistently: grow the listing side, raise your price point, add a specialty, build your brand, strengthen referrals, free up time for clients, or add income beyond your own sales. Most growing agents combine several. At Memorial, each path has tools, training and people behind it, from Listings to Leads and the content lab to mentors and our divisions.

What is the fastest way to grow a real estate business?

For most established agents, it's building on what already works: more listings and more referrals from the people who know you. Both build on each other, since every listing markets you and every happy client refers. A brokerage that handles paperwork and marketing with you also frees up hours you can put back into clients.

How do top real estate agents grow their business?

They focus on listings and referrals, stay visible with consistent marketing, specialize where it makes sense, and hand off work that doesn't need them. They also surround themselves with other high achievers through coaching, masterminds and peers, which is a big part of Memorial's culture.

Should I add a specialty to grow my real estate business?

Often, yes. A specialty such as luxury, international clients or commercial property gives clients a clear reason to choose you and opens new referral sources. You don't have to give up your current clients. At Memorial, our Luxury and International divisions, and KW Commercial Texas, give you a place to learn alongside agents already doing that work.

How can a brokerage help me grow my real estate business?

Through marketing that works for you, training and coaching that keep you improving, support that gives you time back, and a network that sends you referrals. At Memorial, that means Listings to Leads, the content lab, mentors and masterminds, free transaction coordinators, and Keller Williams' global network.
