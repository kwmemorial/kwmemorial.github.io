Tools for international work

The KW network, put to work.

Find agents abroad in KW Command

The Referrals tool in KW Command lets you contact KW agents in other countries and find contact details for any KW region or market center. KW Worldwide publishes a step-by-step referral guide. Commercial agents at KW Commercial Texas work across markets the same way.

Growth Share on international referrals

KW Worldwide notes that international referrals can help you earn Growth Share, in addition to the referral itself.

Events abroad

KW Worldwide holds training and networking events around the world, such as its Mega Camps in Spain, Greece, Portugal, Türkiye and São Paulo, where agents build international referral networks.

HAR global listings

Show clients REALTOR®-listed homes abroad through HAR's Global Information Exchange, and stay with them when they buy outside the U.S.

A multilingual office

Agents across Keller Williams Memorial speak more than 40 languages, so a referral can stay in-house when a client needs a language you don't speak.

Where international meets luxury

Many of our international agents also hold luxury credentials, including CLHMS designees with GUILD™ recognition like our chair, Patricia Uebe, and Roger Aad. That matters when international buyers shop at the top of the market.
